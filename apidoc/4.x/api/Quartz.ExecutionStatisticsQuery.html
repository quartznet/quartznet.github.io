Remarks

Every filter but JobGroup is ExecutionHistoryQuery's and means what it means there, so a chart beside a history page counts the rows that page lists.

A bucket starts at a whole multiple of BucketSize counted from MinValue: an hour bucket starts on the hour and a day bucket at midnight, UTC.
