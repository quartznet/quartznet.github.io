Remarks

Due and Fired are index-aligned, as a TriggersFired(IReadOnlyCollection<IOperableTrigger>, CancellationToken) batch is with its results, and the scheduler reads each pair the way it reads that batch: it runs a fired bundle, releases a trigger that did not fire or failed to, and leaves a declined one alone.

A property left unset reads as an empty list, so a store that fires nothing on acquisition sets Pending alone.

Pending

The acquired triggers not fired yet, in fire-time order. The scheduler waits for the first of them and fires them with TriggersFired(IReadOnlyCollection<IOperableTrigger>, CancellationToken), as it does what AcquireNextTriggers(TriggerAcquisitionRequest, CancellationToken) returns.
