Remarks

OccurrenceUtc and Origin together name the occurrence, and stay the same on every retry of it, on a misfire catch-up of it and on a recovery of it, except a recovery of a retry or a catch-up (see GetOccurrence(IJobExecutionContext)). That is what a consumer that deduplicates per occurrence derives its key from. Quartz formats no key: the format is the consumer's.

Two backfills of one slot have the same OccurrenceUtc, and so does the scheduled firing the slot stands in for, which Origin tells apart from them. The two backfills also share Key; ScheduledFireTimeUtc, when each backfill started its firing, tells them apart.
