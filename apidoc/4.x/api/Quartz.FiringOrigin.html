Enum FiringOrigin

Namespace
Quartz
Assembly
Quartz.dll

Why a firing runs: on its trigger's schedule, because somebody asked for it, or as a backfill of a slot the schedule had.

public enum FiringOrigin

Fields

Backfill = 2

Backfill(IScheduler, TriggerKey, DateTimeOffset, DateTimeOffset, BackfillOptions, CancellationToken) scheduled it for a slot the trigger had. OccurrenceUtc is the slot, not when the backfill ran it.

Manual = 1

TriggerJob(JobKey, JobDataMap?, CancellationToken) or TriggerJob<TInput>(IScheduler, JobKey, TInput, CancellationToken) asked for it. OccurrenceUtc is when the run was asked for.

Schedule = 0

The trigger's schedule called for it. OccurrenceUtc is the fire time the schedule named.

Remarks

Every firing has one, so there is no "none": Schedule is the default. A retry, a misfire catch-up and a recovery keep the origin of the firing they continue.
