Remarks

An extension rather than a member of IJobExecutionContext, so that every hand-written context — a test double, an adapter — keeps compiling, and reads the answer from what it already has.

Remarks

The first that applies decides the origin:

  • Backfill when GetBackfillSlot(IJobExecutionContext) answers a slot. The occurrence is the slot. A backfill of a firing that was itself asked for by hand is a backfill.
  • Manual when the trigger carries ManualTrigger, as the one TriggerJob(JobKey, JobDataMap?, CancellationToken) stores does. The occurrence is ScheduledFireTimeUtc, which for such a trigger is when the run was asked for.
  • Schedule otherwise. The occurrence is ScheduledFireTimeUtc: the same on every retry, and the missed time on a misfire catch-up.

A recovery keeps the origin and occurrence of the firing it runs again: the recovery trigger carries that firing's job data, and is scheduled for the time that firing was, so its ScheduledFireTimeUtc is the original's. One gap: when the firing recovered was itself a retry or a misfire catch-up, the job store recorded the time that attempt was due rather than the occurrence, and the recovery serves that time, with RetryAttempt back at 0.

A firing with no scheduled fire time is taken to be due when it fired.

The markers are read from Trigger's own JobDataMap, never the merged one, so a job whose own data happened to carry one does not make every firing of it look manual or backfilled.

Two backfills of one slot answer the same, and their triggers share a key, because a backfill names its trigger after the slot. What tells them apart is ScheduledFireTimeUtc: the time each backfill started its firing, the same on every retry of it.
