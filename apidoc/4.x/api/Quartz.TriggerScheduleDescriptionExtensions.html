Remarks

An extension rather than a member of ITrigger or of each trigger interface, so that no interface grows and every hand-written trigger keeps compiling. It reads only what each trigger interface already exposes.

The schedule in words, or null for a trigger kind this does not know, such as an ITrigger of your own, and for a cron or recurrence trigger with no expression yet.

Remarks

For display only. The wording may change in any release. Do not parse it, compare it or store it as a key: read the trigger's own members, such as CronExpressionString, for the schedule itself.

  • A cron trigger reads its expression, including L, LW, W, #, the year field and an expression parsed with Unix: At 12:00 on the last day of every month (UTC). A shape it cannot phrase is the expression itself: 0 0-5 9-17 * * ? (UTC).
  • A simple trigger reads its interval and repeat count: Every 5 minutes, 3 times, 30 seconds after each run completes, Once. It has no time zone, so it names none.
  • A calendar-interval trigger reads its interval: Every 2 months (UTC).
  • A daily-time-interval trigger reads its interval, window and days: Every 15 minutes from 09:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday (UTC).
  • A recurrence trigger reads its rule: Monthly on the second Tuesday (UTC). A rule it cannot phrase, such as one with BYSETPOS, is the rule itself.

Every kind but the simple trigger names the time zone it is read in. A trigger with a calendar adds , excluding calendar 'holidays'. The time of day of a calendar-interval or recurrence trigger comes from its start time, which the text does not repeat.
